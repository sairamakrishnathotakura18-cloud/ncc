Frequently Asked Questions by NCC Cadets

How do I claim duty attendance for missed classes during camps?

Submit your official Camp Deputation Letter via this Helpdesk. The Dean Student Welfare automatically dispatches an approved Condonation Slip to your departmental HOD.

What is the minimum attendance required to appear for 'B' & 'C' exams?

Cadets must maintain at least 75% total parade and theory ground attendance across the training academic year, along with attendance at minimum 1 mandatory ATC camp.

What should I do if my digital Cadet ID card has a mistake?

Click "Edit Profile" from the top banner to update your personal details (Roll No, Course, Phone, Email, Photo). For Rank or Battalion changes, submit a ticket to the ANO desk.

How are cadets selected for the Republic Day Parade (RDC) in Delhi?

Selection is conducted through 4 rigorous phases: Battalion trials, Group trials, Inter-Directorate competitions (IGC), and Pre-RDC coaching camps at the DGBNCC facility.
